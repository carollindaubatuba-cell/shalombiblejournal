Shalom

Your quiet place to read, reflect & grow.

A beautiful digital space for Bible study, prayer, journaling, Scripture organization and spiritual reflection — all in one calm, uncluttered place.

Why Shalom

Reading Scripture deserves more than a scroll bar.

Most Bible apps ask you to read and move on. Shalom is built for the slower part — the underlining, the margin notes, the questions you come back to months later. Everything you highlight, write and pray stays gathered in one quiet library that belongs to you.

What's inside

Six spaces, one rhythm

Each one is a real, working part of the app — not a preview.

Inside the app

The reader keeps your margins

Highlights stay on the verse. Notes sit in the margin beside them, the way they would in a journaling Bible.
